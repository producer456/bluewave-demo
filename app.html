Expertise

Autism

"If you've met one person with autism, you've met one person with autism." A neurodevelopmental difference with diverse presentations — not a disease to cure.

90%+of parents reported developmental concerns prior to their child's autism diagnosis.

Why a formal assessment matters

A formal diagnosis from a qualified professional (physician, psychologist, or nurse practitioner) unlocks insurance coverage and support services. School educational assessments are helpful guidance — but they are not medical diagnoses.

The importance of early intervention

Research shows early diagnosis and intervention produce significant improvements in skills and autism-associated behaviors — with the largest effect in younger children.

Strength-based approaches

We focus on each person's abilities and gifts while supporting challenges — in respectful environments where people feel valued and safe.

Expertise

ADHD

A neurodevelopmental difference that impacts regulation of attention, behavior, and impulses. Presentations vary widely — inattentive ADHD is underdiagnosed, particularly in girls.

74–80%heritability of ADHD in family and adoption studies — genetics play a significant role.

Strengths that come with ADHD

  • Creative and innovative thinking
  • High energy channeled into passions
  • Intelligence and curiosity
  • Athletic ability
  • Resilience

Why early assessment matters

  • Tailored support through personalized action plans
  • Improved academic outcomes
  • Better emotional regulation and social skills
  • Prevention of co-occurring conditions
  • Enhanced family dynamics
  • Long-term success skills
  • Reduced stigma

Our treatment approach

Comprehensive evaluation including behavioral strategies, wellness planning, nutritional guidance, lab testing, medication options, and technological aids.

Expertise

Sensory Processing Disorder

SPD occurs when the brain has difficulty receiving, organizing, and responding to sensory information — affecting daily functioning, emotional well-being, and social engagement.

Three primary subtypes

  • Sensory Modulation Disorder — difficulty regulating responses: over- or under-responsive, or seeking excessive input
  • Sensory-Based Motor Disorder — motor coordination and balance challenges
  • Sensory Discrimination Disorder — difficulty distinguishing stimuli; affects spatial awareness and precision

How common is it?

  • Up to 16% of the general population
  • 60% of individuals with ADHD
  • 95% of individuals with autism

Treatment & support

  • Occupational therapy using play-based activities
  • Customized sensory diets
  • Environmental modifications
  • Family training and caregiver support
Expertise

Anxiety & Depression

Anxiety: excessive worry and fear that interferes with daily life — sometimes physical (stomach aches, restlessness). Depression: persistent sadness and loss of interest affecting energy, sleep, concentration, and appetite.

⚠ In crisis? Get help now

National Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988
Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741
Emergencies: 911
Santa Clara County mobile crisis teams available (17&under / 18+).

Warning signs to watch

  • Social withdrawal
  • Self-harm behaviors
  • Substance use
  • Severe mood swings
  • Suicidal ideation
  • Giving away possessions

Support & treatment options

  • Therapy — CBT, DBT, trauma-informed care
  • Medication management
  • Lifestyle support: sleep, nutrition, movement, nature
  • Family and community engagement
  • Crisis intervention

Early intervention matters

Anxiety and depression are highly treatable — early support prevents escalation and builds lifelong coping skills.
